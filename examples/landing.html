Questions

Good questions, short answers

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your phone. Make an account only if you want to sync or join a cheer squad.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing scary. You get a gentle "welcome back" and your rest days keep the streak safe.

Can I export my data?

Yes, as a spreadsheet or a printable poster of your year.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes. Try the moon button at the top of this page.
