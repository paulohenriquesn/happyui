Questions

Good questions, short answers

How far ahead should I order?

For loaves and buns, a day or two ahead is plenty. Birthday cakes need a bit longer, so pick a day with a few days to spare. If it is tight, say so in your note and we will tell you what is possible.

Can I change my order after sending it?

Yes. Just reply to the confirmation e-mail and we will update it.

Do you deliver?

Not at the moment. Everything is for pick-up at the shop, still warm.

What about allergies?

Our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, milk and nuts, so we cannot promise anything is free of them. Tell us about any allergy in your note and we will be honest about what we can do.
